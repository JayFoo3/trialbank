Terms of use

Using Trialbank

Trialbank is a study tool for students preparing for the NSW HSC. Keep your login to yourself and don't share, resell or republish the papers or questions on the site.

Papers and copyright

Past papers belong to NESA and to the schools that wrote them. They're provided here for personal study only. If you own a paper and would like it removed, contact us and we'll take it down.

Auto-marking

Auto-marking uses AI (Anthropic's Claude) to read your working and mark it against the paper's marking guidelines. It's a study aid, not an official mark, and it can make mistakes; always check the feedback against the worked solutions.

Changes and availability

We may update features, access levels and these terms. We'll do our best to keep Trialbank running, but we can't promise it will always be available. If we ever close Trialbank, we'll refund unused bought marks.

Privacy

What we collect

  • Your email address, first name and the subjects you choose.
  • Your answers, scores and progress, so the site can track your weak spots.
  • Photos of working you upload for auto-marking.
  • A record of marking packs you've bought. Card details go directly to Stripe and never reach us.

How it's used and stored

Your information is used only to run Trialbank: your account, your progress and your marking. It's stored with our hosting providers (Supabase for the database and images, Backblaze for paper PDFs). Uploaded working is sent to Anthropic's Claude to be marked. We don't sell your information or use it for advertising.

Your choices

You can ask to see, correct or delete your information at any time; deleting your account removes your progress and uploads. Email us at the contact address above.

The site stores your sign-in and display settings on your device so you stay logged in; it doesn't use advertising or tracking cookies.
